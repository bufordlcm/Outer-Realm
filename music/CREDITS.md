# Music credits

All background music in this folder is free music under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
The tracks were re-encoded to 96 kbps MP3 so they load faster; nothing else was changed.

| Files | Composer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `th-*.mp3` | Tanner Helland | https://github.com/tannerhelland/free-music |
| `gt-*.mp3` | 0xabad1dea (Glorytales) | https://github.com/0xabad1dea/glorytales |

Only the composers' original pieces are included (no covers of other people's music).
The same credits are shown in the app under Game Settings → Sound.
